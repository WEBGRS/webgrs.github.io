    MapLibre GL, Canvas 2D, in-browser video captureMapLibre GL、Canvas 2D、浏览器内录制视频

    In blue, the route in the screenshot: Madison, Reykjavík, Paris蓝线是截图里的那条路线：麦迪逊、雷克雅未克、巴黎

    route-animator · How it works运作方式

    • Flies a vehicle along a multi-stop route over a satellite globe and records it with MediaRecorder. No server ever sees the clip.让交通工具沿着多站路线飞过卫星地球，并用 MediaRecorder 录制，视频不会经过任何服务器。
    • Every vehicle is drawn top-down on canvas, because emoji are three-quarter views and read wrong from directly overhead.每种交通工具都用 canvas 俯视绘制，因为 emoji 是四分之三侧视图，从正上方看会显得别扭。
    • Altitude follows leg-local time rather than distance; keying it to distance let the climb eat a third of the flight.高度按每段航程内的时间变化，而不是按距离；按距离算的话，爬升会吃掉航程的三分之一。

  2. 02 / 06 · Web app, data网页应用 · 数据

    Isthmus Rentals

    605 rentals near campus on one map, every value labeled by where it came from, every gap turned into a question.605 处校园周边的租房放在一张地图上，每个数值都标明来源，每处缺失都变成一个要问的问题。

    Live site在线站点Source源码

    Leaflet, vanilla JS, Python collectors, Madison Metro GTFSLeaflet、原生 JS、Python 采集脚本、Madison Metro GTFS

    • 2,988 units and floor plans from 67 landlords, merged from their own sites and the UW Off-Campus Housing service.2,988 个单元和户型，来自 67 位房东，由他们自己的网站和 UW Off-Campus Housing 服务合并而成。
    • Filters for bedrooms, price per person or per unit, distance from a landmark or your own saved places, utilities, pets and laundry. 305 Madison Metro stops come from the city’s GTFS feed.可按卧室数、每人或整套的价格、到地标或自己保存的地点的距离、水电、宠物和洗衣筛选。305 个 Madison Metro 站点来自市政府的 GTFS 数据。

    Isthmus Rentals · How it works运作方式

    • Every value carries its source: the landlord’s site, the UW list, text read out of a description, or nothing. Unknown stays unknown instead of becoming a plausible guess.每个值都带着来源：房东网站、UW 列表、从描述里读出的文字，或者没有来源。未知就保持未知，不会被一个看似合理的猜测替代。
    • Landlords quote rent per person or per unit and rarely say which. Each price is tagged with its evidence; when none settles it, the price is read as whichever puts one person between $800 and $1,500.房东报价有的按人、有的按整套，而且很少说明是哪种。每个价格都标有依据；依据都不成立时，就按能让一个人的租金落在 $800 到 $1,500 之间的那种理解来读。
    • Phone and email of individual landlords who appear only on the UW list stay off the page; it links to that listing instead.只出现在 UW 列表上的个人房东，电话和邮箱不会出现在页面上，页面改为链接到那条列表。

    Isthmus Rentals · What the data says数据说了什么

    • Closer costs more, and not just because of bedrooms: median rent per person is $1,200 within 0.4 mi of Bascom Hall and $978 at 1–1.25 mi.离校园越近越贵，而且不只是卧室数的缘故：Bascom Hall 0.4 英里内的人均租金中位数是 $1,200，在 1 到 1.25 英里处是 $978。
    • Each property is one point, its median listing; shared rooms are left out. The rank correlation is −0.29 once each rent is divided by the median for its bedroom count (permutation p < 0.001).每处房源只算一个点，取它的中位数报价；合租间不计入。把每个租金除以同卧室数的中位数之后，秩相关为 −0.29（置换检验 p < 0.001）。

    Madison Eats

    2,292 places to eat in Dane County, scored from six rating sources, with the delivery apps that carry each one.2,292 家戴恩县的餐饮店，按六个评分来源打分，并标出各家上了哪些外卖平台。

    Live site在线站点Source源码

    Python collectors, vanilla JS, Leaflet, Cloudflare WorkersPython 采集脚本、原生 JS、Leaflet、Cloudflare Workers

    • Photo cards, a map and a delivery-app tab, searchable in English or Chinese, with filters for cuisine, occasion and open now. Each place shows highlights such as its rank within its cuisine.照片卡片、地图和外卖平台页，支持中英文搜索，可按菜系、场合和营业中筛选。每家店会显示几条亮点，比如它在本菜系中的排名。
    • 1,160 places can be ordered on at least one app: DoorDash 859, Uber Eats 817, Grubhub 539, EatStreet 274, Toast 182.1,160 家至少能在一个平台下单：DoorDash 859、Uber Eats 817、Grubhub 539、EatStreet 274、Toast 182。

    Madison Eats · How it works运作方式

    • County food licences and OpenStreetMap give the base list. Collectors for six rating sources, county inspections and r/madisonwi are linked to it by name and address.县里的食品经营许可和 OpenStreetMap 构成基础清单。六个评分来源、县卫生检查和 r/madisonwi 的采集结果，按名称和地址关联到清单上。
    • Sources grade on different curves (Toast averages 4.8 stars, EatStreet 4.0), so each rating becomes a percentile among Madison places on the same app, and places with few ratings are pulled toward the middle.各来源的打分尺度不同（Toast 平均 4.8 星，EatStreet 4.0），所以每个评分会换算成它在同一平台上麦迪逊店铺中的百分位，评分很少的店会被拉向中间。
    • The public page carries no data. A Cloudflare Worker returns one page or one place at a time, behind a human check, daily quotas and a request log.公开页面里没有数据。Cloudflare Worker 一次只返回一页或一家店，并设有人机验证、每日配额和请求日志。

    Madison Eats · What the data says数据说了什么

    • The sources agree only loosely. Rank correlations between pairs of sources run from 0.26 to 0.61 for the same places, and Uber Eats and Toast are uncorrelated. The score gives a source more weight when it agrees with the others.各来源之间只是大致一致。同一批店铺在每两个来源之间的秩相关从 0.26 到 0.61，Uber Eats 和 Toast 之间几乎不相关。打分时，和其他来源越一致的来源权重越高。
    • Checked against a signal left out of the score, a random award winner outranks a random non-winner 66% of the time with the award bonus removed (168 winners).用一个未参与打分的信号来检验：去掉奖项加分后，随机挑一家获奖店和一家未获奖店，获奖店排名更靠前的概率是 66%（168 家获奖店）。

  4. 04 / 06 · Web app + API网页应用 + API

    uw-course-lookup

    Grade history, instructor GPAs and seats for all 4,438 Fall 2026 UW–Madison courses, plus a timetable planner that knows your degree.4,438 门 Fall 2026 UW–Madison 课程的成绩历史、教师 GPA 和座位，还有一个了解你学位要求的课表规划器。

    Live site在线站点Source源码

    Vanilla JS, Cloudflare Workers and Workers AI, SQLite, GitHub Actions CI原生 JS、Cloudflare Workers 与 Workers AI、SQLite、GitHub Actions CI

    • A sticky A–F band morphs into whichever course you hover, with ticks marking the campus-wide split, so every row reads against the baseline.顶部的 A–F 条会变形成你悬停的那门课的分布，刻度线标出全校的整体分布，每一行都能和基准比较。
    • Lenses such as Instructor matters, a dot plot of every instructor’s GPA, saved plans, side-by-side compare, and an assistant that can search courses and build a timetable.有 教师影响大 这样的筛选视角、每位教师的 GPA 点图、保存的计划、并排对比，以及能搜课、能排课表的助手。

    uw-course-lookup · The planner课表规划器

    • Pick one of 212 degree programs and drop in your transcript or DARS. The file is read in the browser, not uploaded, and the planner shows what is done, in progress and left.选一个学位专业（共 212 个），导入成绩单或 DARS。文件只在浏览器里读取，不会上传，规划器会显示哪些已完成、哪些在修、哪些还差。
    • It searches for conflict-free timetables over each course’s real enrollment packages (a lecture plus its discussion), honoring early starts, days off and seat availability, and returns six options with an .ics export.它在每门课真实的选课组合（一节讲课加配套的讨论课）上搜索没有冲突的课表，遵守不早起、空出整天、座位是否有空等偏好，给出六个方案，并能导出 .ics。
    • If the request is impossible it says why, for example two required courses that always overlap.如果要求无法满足，它会说明原因，比如两门必修课的时间总是重叠。

    uw-course-lookup · How it works运作方式

    • The page holds no course data. A Cloudflare Worker serves one page of results at a time to a browser that has passed an invisible human check.页面本身不含课程数据。Cloudflare Worker 一次只给通过了隐形人机验证的浏览器返回一页结果。
    • Each visitor and each network has a daily allowance counted in course records. Requests are rate-limited and logged, never the assistant conversation, and CI fails if a private file is committed.每位访客和每个网络每天有一份按课程条数计算的额度。请求会被限流和记录（从不记录助手对话），而且只要有私有文件被提交，CI 就会失败。
    • No endpoint returns the dataset in bulk. That raises the cost of copying everything; it cannot prevent it.没有任何接口能一次返回整套数据。这会抬高整体拷贝的成本，但并不能杜绝。

    uw-course-lookup · What the data says数据说了什么

    • Instructor beats course. In MATH 320 the most and least generous instructors, each with 200+ graded students, sit 1.67 GPA points apart on the same syllabus.教师比课程更重要。在 MATH 320 里，最宽松和最严格的教师（各有 200 多名有成绩的学生）在同一份大纲下 GPA 相差 1.67 分。
    • How often a course comes up in chat says nothing about its grade (ρ = −0.07, n = 215). The tone of its Reddit threads does (ρ = +0.43, n = 220).一门课在群聊里被提到的次数和它的成绩无关（ρ = −0.07，n = 215），而它在 Reddit 帖子里的口碑有关（ρ = +0.43，n = 220）。

    photo-organizer

    Sorts a 310 GB travel photo library into people, scenery, and per-trip clusters, entirely on a local GPU. Nothing is uploaded.在本地 GPU 上，把 310 GB 的旅行照片库分成人像、风景和按旅行划分的聚类，全程不上传任何东西。

    Source源码

    CLIP ViT-B/32, HDBSCAN, PyTorch with CUDA, FastAPI. 7,346 files, 0 errors.CLIP ViT-B/32、HDBSCAN、使用 CUDA 的 PyTorch、FastAPI。7,346 个文件，0 个错误。

    • Zero-shot classifies people vs. scenery, then clusters scenery by embedding into trip-scale groups per region.用零样本分类区分人像和风景，再按嵌入向量把风景聚成每个地区的旅行级分组。
    • RAW and XMP sidecars follow their JPEG through every move; the source tree is copy-only and never mutated.RAW 文件和 XMP 附属文件会跟着对应的 JPEG 一起移动；源目录只做复制，从不改动。

    photo-organizer · How it works运作方式

    • Embedding cache keyed by path + size + mtime, so a rerun over 7k files costs minutes instead of hours.嵌入缓存以路径、大小和修改时间为键，所以对 7 千个文件重跑只要几分钟，而不是几小时。
    • Local FastAPI web UI for browsing, renaming, and re-filing, reachable from a phone over LAN.本地 FastAPI 网页界面，可以浏览、改名和重新归档，还能通过局域网在手机上访问。

Decisions I'd defend我会坚持的决定

Show the gap, don't fake the data.如实展示缺口，不要伪造数据。

Connecticut replaced its eight counties with planning regions in 2022, and the two do not partition each other. DataMap renders the old counties as no data rather than inventing a crosswalk that would look correct and be wrong.康涅狄格州在 2022 年用规划区取代了原来的八个县，两套边界并不互相对应。DataMap 把旧的县显示为 无数据，而不是编一张看起来正确、实际上错误的对照表。

Aggregates leave the machine; records don't.离开这台电脑的是汇总，不是原始记录。

The course tool is built on a private chat archive. Only anonymized counts are published; the archive itself is gitignored and stays on one disk. A useful dataset is not a licence to ship it.课程工具建立在一份私人聊天记录之上。公开的只有匿名化的计数，记录本身被 gitignore，只留在一块硬盘上。数据有用，并不意味着可以随便发布。

Public repos are original work only.公开仓库只放原创作品。

Graded coursework stays off GitHub, no exceptions. What's public is what I designed, and I can walk through every line of it.计分的课程作业不上 GitHub，没有例外。公开的都是我自己设计的东西，每一行我都能讲清楚。
